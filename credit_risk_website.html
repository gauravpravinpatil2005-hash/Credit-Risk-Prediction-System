// 03 · Evaluation Metrics

How we score
each model

Five complementary metrics — because accuracy alone is meaningless on a 14:1 imbalanced dataset.

ROC-AUC
AUC
Area under the ROC curve — primary ranking metric. Measures discrimination across all thresholds.
F1-Score
F1
Harmonic mean of precision & recall. Critical for imbalanced classes where FP and FN costs differ.
Precision
PPV
Of predicted defaults, how many are true defaults? Minimises costly false alarms.
Recall
TPR
Of actual defaults, how many were caught? Minimises missed defaults — the bigger credit risk.
Accuracy
ACC
Overall correctness. Supplementary on imbalanced data but still reported for completeness.
